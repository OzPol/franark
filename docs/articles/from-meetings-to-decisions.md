# From meeting notes to decisions

Document ID: ARTICLE-001. Template version: 0.1.0. Status: initial design article.

A useful meeting workflow separates what was said, what was agreed, and what
the team proposes next. An explicit commitment can become a task candidate.
A suspected improvement is a recommendation that needs supporting evidence.
A missing owner or deadline remains a question.

Franark passes those records through investigation, operational drafting, and
review. FRAN organizes the priorities using the approved project criteria and
communicates the decisions needed. The management draft should answer four
questions: what did we find, what supports it, what are the options, and what
decision is required?

For an IT assessment, invoices establish spending. Contracts, inventories, and
service history establish what that spending buys. Comparing these together
produces a meaningful quote request and vendor comparison.

For a WellSky issue, the useful output is a diagnostic packet: the affected
workflow, available evidence, candidate explanations, exact checks, and the
interpretation of each possible result. Where the application is unavailable,
the packet gives Oz a precise observation to make.
